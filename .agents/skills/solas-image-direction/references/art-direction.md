# The Solas Guide art direction

## Visual register

Editorial travel and architecture photography with documentary restraint. Calm, specific, grounded, and quietly authoritative. The image should feel observed rather than staged.

## Asset families

- Hero: architecture or landscape leads; human presence is small, incidental, and not looking at camera; leave usable negative space for interface copy.
- Practitioner: environmental portrait of a fictional concept subject; natural posture, available light, credible working space, no performance for camera.
- Place: relationship between architecture, vegetation, climate, craft, and daily use; avoid resort-advertising polish.
- Practice: hands, materials, tools, or preparation shown with cultural care; avoid implying medical outcomes.
- Editorial detail: teak, stone, woven fibres, water, foliage, thresholds, shadows, and signs of use.

## Palette and light

Use cream, warm stone, weathered teak, restrained terracotta, deep tropical green, soft charcoal, and muted water tones. Use overcast daylight, window light, or shaded tropical daylight. Retain believable shadow detail and natural skin texture.

## Camera language

Use full-frame editorial photography, 35mm or 50mm perspective, eye-level or restrained architectural framing, moderate depth of field, and subtle grain. Keep verticals plausible. Avoid ultra-wide distortion, HDR, bloom, teal-orange grading, and excessive bokeh.

## Avoid

Stock wellness photography; posed yoga or meditation; direct-to-camera smiles in heroes; hands on hearts; prayer gestures; golden-hour silhouettes; levitation, aura, smoke, crystals, fantasy light, or mystical particles; generic white spa interiors; decorative cultural stereotypes; flawless CGI surfaces; oversaturation; over-sharpening; text, logos, or watermarks.
